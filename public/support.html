Answers to common questions, and how to reach us.

Getting started

How do I add a subscription?

On the Wall tab, tap the wall to walk up to it, then tap any empty slot (the squares with a +). Search the catalog, or pick from Popular in your region, then fill in the price, currency, how often it renews and the next payment date, and tap Add. The tile goes into the slot you tapped.

My service isn’t in the catalog. Can I still add it?

Yes. Tap Custom in the catalog and type any name: rent, a gym, a newspaper, anything that charges you regularly. Choose a tile color, and if you like, pick a photo from your library as its logo. Otherwise the tile shows the name’s first letters.

Why does a tile say “Add price”?

Services you pick during setup are added without a price, because prices differ by country and plan. Walk up to the wall, tap the tile, tap Details and enter what you actually pay. Until then, it counts as zero in your totals.

How do I move tiles around?

Walk up to the wall and drag a tile onto another slot. An outline shows where it will land. Drop it on an empty slot to move it, or on another tile to swap the two.

How do I edit or delete a subscription?

Walk up to the wall, tap the tile and tap Details to change anything, including the price and the next payment. To delete it, tap Delete subscription at the bottom of its details. Subwall asks first, because deleting removes the tile, its reminders and its history.

What’s the difference between Paused, Cancelled and deleting?

In a subscription’s details, Status can be Active, Paused or Cancelled. Paused and cancelled tiles stay on the wall, faded, and stop counting towards what’s due. Cancelled subscriptions also show in History with how much you’ve saved since. Deleting removes the subscription completely, history included.

What is the “Paid with” field?

It’s an optional note of what each subscription is charged to, such as “Visa •1234” or “PayPal”. Subwall shows it in the reminder (“$15.49 will be charged to Visa •1234”), on the tile’s card and on the Calendar’s Due this week card, so you know which account to top up. Please don’t enter full card numbers or security codes: a nickname is all it needs.

The wall and calendar

What do “This month” and “Due this week” show?

This month is the total of every payment in the current month. Due this week is the total for today and the next six days, with the names of what’s due. Both sit above your tiles in the room view; tap either one to open the Calendar tab.

Why do some tiles have a yellow border?

A yellow border means that subscription has a payment due this week: today or in the next six days. They’re the same subscriptions the Due this week panel counts. The border goes away by itself once the payment date has passed.

How do I get to the calendar?

Swipe sideways on the wall, or tap Calendar in the switch at the bottom. The room slides along to a paper calendar, with payment days circled in each service’s color, and a sticky note of what’s due this week. Tap either one to open the Calendar tab, then tap any day to see what renews.

What happens when my wall is full?

A wall holds 20 tiles. With Subwall Pro, once every slot is used a second wall of 20 appears where the calendar was, and the calendar moves further along. Swipe from wall to wall and on to the calendar; the dots above the switch show where you are. On the free plan you can have up to 12 subscriptions, so the first wall is enough.

What does the light switch do?

Tap the light switch on the wall, left of your tiles, or use Settings → Room lights. With the lights off, the room goes dark with a spotlight over your totals, and the whole app switches to dark mode. With the lights on, Subwall follows your iPhone’s Light or Dark setting again.

Can I see my subscriptions as a list instead?

Yes. Turn on Settings → Show wall as a list for a plain list sorted by next payment. The list also appears automatically when VoiceOver is on. Turn the setting off to go back to the wall.

What’s behind the bell?

The bell at the top of the wall opens Notifications: what renews in the next two weeks, renewals and price changes from the last 30 days, and your default reminder. A dot on the bell means something is due in the next few days.

Reminders

When do reminders arrive?

By default, one day before each payment at 9:00 AM. In Settings, choose Remind me on the day, 1, 2 or 3 days before, or a week before, and pick the time with At. Each subscription can also have its own reminder, or none, in its details.

Can Subwall remind me before a free trial ends?

Yes. In a subscription’s details, turn on Free trial and set Trial ends. Subwall reminds you two days before, so you have time to cancel, and doesn’t send renewal reminders until the trial is over.

My reminders don’t arrive. What can I do?

Check these, in order:

  • In iOS Settings → Notifications → Subwall, Allow Notifications is on.
  • In Subwall’s Settings, Reminders is on and the time is right.
  • The subscription is Active, and its own reminder isn’t turned off in its details.
  • A Focus mode, such as Sleep, isn’t silencing notifications at that time.

Open Subwall once after changing a setting, so it can schedule the next reminders. iOS lets an app keep 64 reminders waiting at a time, so Subwall schedules the soonest ones and adds more each time you open it.

Money and currencies

I pay in more than one currency. How are totals shown?

Each subscription keeps its own currency, and totals list each currency separately, like “$42.97 + €9.99”. Subwall doesn’t convert between currencies, because that would need live exchange rates from the internet and the numbers would only ever be estimates. Choose your Main currency in Settings: it’s listed first, and it’s the one the History chart shows.

A service raised its price. What should I do?

Open the subscription’s details and enter the new price. Subwall records the change, so it shows in History and Notifications, and your earlier payments keep their old price. (Changes are recorded when the currency stays the same.)

How does History work?

History shows what you’ve spent month by month, by category, and recent activity: price changes, and cancellations with how much you’ve saved since. It’s worked out from each subscription’s start date, price and renewals. The free plan shows the last 3 months; 1Y and All come with Subwall Pro. When you add a subscription, turn on I know when I started paying to include earlier payments.

How do I cancel a subscription with the service?

Subwall can’t cancel anything for you: it never connects to your accounts. For many services from the catalog, the subscription’s details have a Manage or cancel link to that service’s own page. After you’ve cancelled there, set the subscription’s Status to Cancelled in Subwall, so History can count what you save.

Subwall Pro and purchases

What does Subwall Pro include?

Unlimited subscriptions (with a new wall every 20), full history and charts, six room themes, Face ID lock and extra app icons. You can get Pro three ways: Pro Yearly, with a 7-day free trial; Pro Monthly; or Lifetime, a one-time purchase. All three include exactly the same features.

How does the free trial work?

Pro Yearly starts with 7 days free, once per Apple ID. When your trial starts, Subwall adds a Subwall Pro tile to your wall and reminds you 2 days before the trial ends. If you don’t cancel at least 24 hours before it ends, the yearly plan starts and Apple charges you. If you’ve already used your free week, the button says Subscribe instead. Pro Monthly and Lifetime have no free trial.

How do I cancel Pro Yearly or Pro Monthly?

In Subwall, go to Settings and tap Manage subscription. You can also open iOS Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions and Subwall. Cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date to avoid the next charge. Apple handles all subscription billing.

What happens when Pro ends?

Pro stays on until the end of the period you paid for. After that, every subscription stays on your wall and keeps its reminders. You can’t add more than 12, History goes back to the last 3 months, Pro rooms go back to Sky, and Face ID lock turns off. Your room choice is remembered, so it comes back if you get Pro again.

I bought Pro but it isn’t active. What do I do?

Go to Settings in Subwall and tap Restore purchases, signed in with the same Apple ID you used to buy it. This also brings Pro to a new iPhone.

How do I get a refund?

Refunds are handled by Apple. Visit reportaproblem.apple.com, sign in, and choose the Subwall purchase.

Themes, icons, sharing and Face ID

How do I change the room?

Go to Settings → Pro features → Room theme. Sky is free; Sage, Blush, Dune, Midnight, Lilac and Blocks come with Subwall Pro. Each card shows a preview of that room. The light switch works in every room.

How do I change the app icon?

With Subwall Pro, go to Settings → Pro features → App icon and choose Sky, Night or Amber. iOS shows a short message to confirm the change.

How do I share my wall?

Tap the share button at the top of the Wall tab. Subwall makes a picture of your tiles on your room’s wallpaper. Prices are hidden unless you turn on Show prices. Tap Share to post it or send it. If you choose Save Image, iOS asks once for permission to add photos to your library.

How does Face ID lock work?

With Subwall Pro, turn on Settings → Pro features → Face ID lock. Subwall then locks whenever you leave the app, and asks for Face ID (or your passcode) when you come back. Your face data never leaves your iPhone; Subwall only learns whether it matched.

Is there a dark mode?

Yes. Subwall follows your iPhone’s Light or Dark setting. You can also switch the room’s lights off with the switch on the wall or Settings → Room lights, which puts the whole app in dark mode.

Your data

Where are my subscriptions stored?

Only on your iPhone. Subwall has no account, no server and no connection to your bank, so we never see what you track. See the Privacy Policy for details.

How do I move to a new iPhone?

Subwall’s data is included in your regular iPhone backup. Restore your new iPhone from an iCloud or computer backup, or use Quick Start, and your wall comes along. Then tap Restore purchases in Settings to bring back Subwall Pro.

Does Subwall sync between devices?

Not yet. Each device keeps its own subscriptions, because Subwall has no account or server. Subwall Pro does carry over: on the other device, tap Restore purchases in Settings, signed in with the same Apple ID.

How do I delete my data?

Delete any subscription from its details with Delete subscription. To remove everything at once, go to Settings → Danger Zone → Reset All Data, type CONFIRM and tap Reset Everything. That deletes every subscription, price change, reminder and all your history, and shows the welcome screens again; your settings are kept. Deleting the app also erases everything it stored. Nothing is kept anywhere else, and your Subwall Pro purchase stays with your Apple ID.
